HOVER LANGUAGE
Everything interactive lifts translate(−3px, −3px) and drops a hard offset shadow — no blur, no glow. Shadow is ink on the red and bone grounds, accent on the dark panels where ink would vanish. Skewed chrome keeps its skew in the hover transform.
TEXTURE & RULES
Halftone — radial-gradient dots, 7px grid, 0.085 opacity (0.035 on CALM)
Skew is −7° on chrome, with a +7° counter-skew on the label so text stays upright. Diagonal fields are clip-path polygons, never rotated boxes. Hit targets never below 44px. Body copy never below 14px. Icons are inline stroke SVG on a 24px grid — no emoji.
