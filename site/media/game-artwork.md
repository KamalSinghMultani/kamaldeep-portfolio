# Game-menu artwork

Generated using the built-in image-generation tool. Originals copied unchanged into the project.

- `menu-character.png`: a two-pose atlas of an original engineer avatar; left holds a tablet, right offers a blank card. The lenticular renderer samples the two halves.
- `support-story.png`: three equal-width illustrated support-workflow panels. CSS selects each panel without modifying the original file.

These are decorative, illustrative assets, not photos or evidence of actual workplace incidents. No flags, crests, brand marks, insignia, or seals were requested.

## Character prompt

Create a production artwork texture atlas, NOT a UI mockup. Exactly two equal portrait panels touching side by side, landscape total ratio 3:2, each panel portrait 3:4. Same ORIGINAL young adult male anime software/cloud engineer with tousled dark hair, wearing a plain dark unbranded jacket, confident thoughtful expression. Left panel holding a tablet at chest height, looking slightly toward viewer's left. Right panel same character, identical face scale and eye/head positioning, slightly different confident expression and offering an unmarked ivory calling card near chest height. Both chest-up, portrait centered within its own half; head fully visible with generous upper margin. Premium angular hand-inked manga illustration, detailed fine linework, warm ivory highlights, black shadows, crimson rim lighting, extremely subtle gold light, sophisticated Persona-inspired editorial palette. Almost solid background #101010 blending to pure #101010 at every edge; no background scenery. No text, no symbols, no crests, no insignia, no flags, no logos, no badges, no jewelry emblems, no headbands. Clean shoulders fading gently into dark at bottom. Each half is a separate complete portrait, same aligned composition for a two-view lenticular display. Stylish cinematic illustration that will sit on the right of a game-like portfolio menu. Full bleed.

## Support workflow prompt

Create one landscape 3:1 triptych of three equal square manga illustration panels, edge to edge with NO borders and NO text. Same original dark-haired young adult male IT technician in plain unbranded dark jacket in all three. Panel 1 left: listens thoughtfully with headset at a workstation with a simple warning triangle on monitor, identifying a user's issue. Panel 2 middle: examining laptop and network device cables, focused diagnosis and repair. Panel 3 right: documenting completed work at a healthy monitor, small checkmark icon on screen. Style: premium hand-inked manga, refined angular drawing, warm ivory, charcoal black and crimson, restrained gold highlights. Backgrounds dark #101010, clear silhouettes, readable process at small sizes. No flags, crests, seals, symbols on clothing, brand logos, text, gibberish code, official insignia, or watermarks. It will be a labelled illustrated workflow on a professional game-menu portfolio, labels added in HTML. Each panel subject remains centered within its square; no objects crossing into adjacent panels.
