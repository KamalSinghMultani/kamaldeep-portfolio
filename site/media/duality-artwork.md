# Duality artwork

Generated with the built-in image-generation tool for Part 1 of the portfolio.

Asset: `duality-atlas.png` (1536 × 1024 PNG). The left half is Kakashi; the right
half is Obito. Both halves are sampled directly from this one image by the hero.
The CSS fallback uses background positioning; the WebGL implementation samples
the two halves through virtual lenslets. The original generated image was copied
unchanged into this directory.

The atlas is external to the generated HTML. Include `media/duality-atlas.png`
when serving or publishing `site/index.html`.

## Final generation prompt

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: a two-frame texture atlas for a mouse-controlled lenticular anime portfolio hero. Create ONE landscape image, precisely TWO equally sized portrait panels side by side, touching at exactly x=50%, no gutter, no border, no text, no lettering, no watermarks. Left half: Kakashi Hatake from Naruto, silver spiky hair, headband, black lower-face mask, visible red Sharingan eye. Right half: adult unmasked Obito Uchiha from Naruto, short spiky black hair, scarred right side of his face, red Sharingan eye, dark high-collar outfit. Both are chest-up portraits with EXACTLY MATCHED front-facing centered composition, head scale, eye height, shoulder position and dramatic direct gaze; each face centered horizontally in its own half. Hair fits fully within each panel, crown near 10% height, eyes at 39% height, chin near 64%, shoulders fill lower third. They are independent full portraits, NOT two halves of one face. Style: premium hand-inked manga cover, exquisite angular ink drawing, strong silhouette, graphic cel shadows, fine etched halftone, expressive detailed eyes; dramatic screenprinted crimson #d92020, warm ivory #f2ede1, deep black #0b0b0d, restrained metallic gold highlights. Each panel has an identical almost-black background with sparse crimson diagonal speed strokes behind shoulders. High contrast ivory faces/hair against black, dramatic red rimlight, sophisticated intense mature anime art direction. Flat artwork ready for a shader, NOT a photo of a card, NOT a website mockup, no existing holographic stripes or glare baked in, no 3D perspective; both panels square-on. Landscape overall ratio 3:2, each individual portrait panel ratio 3:4. Full bleed art.
